# Phones and watches

Read first, then act: `phone_read` or `watch_read` give the state and the deviceId; `phone_control`, `watch_control` and the phone's capabilities in `device_control` change it. What belongs to a phone's own person is private, so answers about someone else's phone leave it out; say so instead of guessing.

## Phones and watches

`phone_read` or `watch_read` first, then `phone_control` or `watch_control`. `phone_read` with view context says what a person is doing; with view detail and the phone's deviceId (from `device_find`) it gives battery and charging, sound and now playing, screen, signal and data use, storage, apps and notifications, next alarm; name the sections wanted to keep it short. What belongs to the phone's own person (apps, notification titles, Wi-Fi name, what is playing) is left out when asking about someone else's phone, so say that instead of guessing. The same cut applies to `device_read`, `history_read`, `event_find` and `device_find` for someone else's phone: place names, what is playing, screen time and what the phone is doing come back empty or missing for other people. Controlling a phone that is not the person's own is refused for members, and opening an app on a phone works only for its own person, even for the owner; report the refusal as given. Brightness, ringer, volumes, Do Not Disturb and the flashlight are also readable and settable as the phone's capabilities, and so are the screen timeout (`screen_timeout`, seconds as text from 15 to 1800), `auto_rotate`, `haptic_feedback` (touch vibration) and `touch_sounds`; these need the phone's Modify system settings permission, and a refusal naming it is reported as given. `wifi_on`, `bluetooth_on`, `location_on`, `airplane_mode` and `power_save` are read-only: no app can switch Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, location, airplane mode, NFC, battery saver or dark mode on Android, so read the state and, only on the person's own phone, send `open_settings` (a capability for `device_control`, or the `phone_control` action of that name) with the page `wifi`, `bluetooth`, `internet`, `location`, `airplane`, `nfc`, `battery_saver`, `display`, `sound`, `dnd_access`, `write_settings` or `app_details` to bring up the right Settings screen, then say plainly that the person must tap the switch themselves. The phone may show a notification to tap instead of opening it at once. Never promise to switch those, and never pass a raw setting name or intent; the pages are a closed list. Finding a phone rings at full volume even on silent; stop it with the same action and on false. `phone_control` with receive_file and a chat attachment's id saves that file on the phone (Downloads/SmartEverything); computers take files through `file_edit` upload. Watch actions run through its phone and use the sports it reports. A watch's workout is also a device value: `watch_workout` reads none, running, paused or finished, `watch_workout_start` starts one of the sports it reports and `watch_workout_control` takes pause, resume or finish, so automations can drive the watch with a device step.

## Machine actions

Machine actions are capabilities, not extra tools: ring, stop ringing, open an app, open a link or file, lock, and show the unlock prompt on a phone or computer. `device_read` lists only those the machine reports as usable and says which permission a missing one needs; send them with `device_control` and report a refusal as given. Unlock on a computer waits for the person's approval on their phone and is refused if nobody approves; a phone cannot be unlocked, only woken to its PIN prompt. Screenshots of a phone or computer use `sight_look`. A refusal names the permission to grant; tell the person where.
