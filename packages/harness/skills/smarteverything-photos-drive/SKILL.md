# Photos and Drive

Every person has one personal drive (My Drive) in the hub's data folder, and the whole home shares one Shared Drive. Nobody sees another person's drive unless they shared an item with them. Photos is the same files seen as pictures and videos; Drive is the same files seen as folders. `drive_read` reads and `drive_edit` changes. Both take `personId` from `person_read`, but it must be the person you are talking to (left out means them): a drive is never read or changed for someone else, and that holds for the owner's AI too. The owner's AI reaches the owner's own drive, the Shared Drive and what was shared with the owner; for other people it can read only their space totals in `usage`. A refusal on a file that is not shared is final; say so and do not look for another route.

In the app, Photos is one Library page with a search bar and tabs: All, Favourites, Videos, Screenshots, Cameras, Made with AI and On this device. The Library holds the photos and videos under `Photos/` in the person’s own drive and the Shared Drive pictures that were kept there; photos are private to their person unless shared, the owner included. On this device is app-only: it shows this device’s local photos and videos (Pictures and Videos on desktop, the permitted media library on Android) without requiring backup, so `drive_read` never lists them. Backup is optional and copies originals into `Photos/` of the personal drive; its settings, and Duplicates, live in Settings › Photos. There are no memories: trip albums are an app feature only. Photos also has a Folders page that lists the phone albums, computer folders and Drive folders the photos come from; a folder can be hidden from the Library and the other photo views (search, people, places) without touching its backup, and a Show all switch in the Library brings hidden folders back for the session. Hidden folders are listed in Settings › Photos › Hidden folders.

With Smart features on, the hub groups faces into people and pets (faces never leave the hub) and, with Smart search and tags on, OpenRouter reads previews so the hub keeps a caption, labels, text written in the photo and a meaning index. Photos also has Sharing (shared albums and a partner), Archive and a Locked folder. Another person's photos, faces, groups and activity are never reachable, the owner's AI included; the owner sees only counts and spend per person in `smart_status`.

## Read before you change

`drive_read` takes a `view`:

- `drives` lists the drives you can open (your own and the Shared Drive) with state, size and health. `folder` lists one folder (`rootId` from `drives`, optional `folderId`, `sortField` with `sortDirection`, `cursor` for the next page). `item` gives one file or folder.
- `shared_with_me` lists the files and folders other people shared with you, with your role (view or edit) and who shared them. `shared_by_me` lists what you shared, with the people and the links.
- `search` finds files by `text`, `kinds` (image, video, audio, document, other), `starred`, `from` and `to` (days taken), inside a drive (`rootId`) or `folderId`, sorted by `sortField` and `sortDirection`. `match` all (the default) looks in names and inside files, including text the hub read out of documents and out of pictures; `match` names looks in names only. Every hit says where the words were found (`where` name, text or image_text) and gives a snippet with the words marked between the control characters U+0002 and U+0003.
- `library` is the Photos Library: without `month` it lists the months with counts, with `month` (YYYY-MM) one month page (`cursor` for the next page). It narrows by `libraryFilter` (all, favourites, videos, screenshots), `text`, `from` and `to` (days taken), `placeId`, `countryCode`, `camera`, `albumId` and `folderKey` (one folder's photos, hidden or not). Photos of hidden folders are left out unless `allFolders` is true.
- `photo_folders` lists the folders the person's photos come from: phone albums (`phone:` keys), computer folders (`computer:` keys) and Drive folders (`drive:` keys), each with its name, where it is, the count, the newest photo and whether it is hidden.
- `albums` (`surface` photos or drive) and `album` (`albumId`) list albums and their files. `places` lists where photos were taken, with `placeId` one place. `stacks` lists bursts and RAW plus JPEG pairs, with `stackId` one stack.
- `duplicates` groups files with identical content, `trash` lists what will leave for good and when, `recent` lists what was opened, added or changed, and the shortcuts made (`filter` everything, opened, added or changed; added and everything include new shortcuts).
- `versions` lists the earlier versions of a file (`itemId`), `shares` the links made (optional `itemId`), `usage` your space (the owner also gets one totals row per person, never names of files), `stats` your drives by kind and folder, `health` whether files are intact and backed up, `placements` the synced folders and the mounted My Drive on your computers with their state, `conflicts` the sync conflicts waiting, `backup` the backup progress of each device and how much room is left for it.

- `zip` (`itemId` of a .zip file, `cursor`) lists what is inside the archive in its own order: path, size, packed size, whether it is encrypted. `text` (`itemId`) gives the text the hub read out of a document or a picture, with its state (ok, empty, failed, unsupported or pending); a view only file refuses it.
- `comments` (`itemId`, `cursor`) lists the comment threads on a file or folder with their replies, oldest first. `suggested` lists the files you probably want, each with the reason (you opened it, you edited it, someone edited it, shared with you, a new comment, you were mentioned). `notifications` is your Drive inbox, newest first.
- `access_requests` with `box` waiting (the default) lists the requests waiting for you to answer, with `box` made the ones you sent; with `itemId` it tells whether you can open that item or may ask for it. `file_requests` lists the upload links you made (`folderId` narrows to one folder), and with `requestId` the files one of them received and who sent each. `transfers` lists the ownership transfers you offered or were offered. `offline` lists the files kept offline on your phones (`clientId` narrows to one phone).

- People and pets: `people` (optional `groupKind` person or pet) lists the groups the hub found, each with a name (null until named), `linkedProfileId`, a cover, the count and whether it is hidden, plus `mergeSuggestions` (two groups that may be one person). `person` (`groupId`) gives the group and the months of their photos; with `month` it gives that month page (`cursor` for the next). `item_faces` (`itemId`) gives the faces and pets found in one photo with the group each belongs to, and `analysis` with what was found in it: caption, scene, labels and the text read from it.
- Finding photos by content: `library` (and `archive`, with the same fields) also narrows by `groupIds` (photos showing every one of these people or pets), `labels` (things such as dog or beach, every one), `photoText` (words written in the photo) and `meaning` (describe what the photo shows in plain words; works when Smart search is on). For “Sam at the beach”, read `people` for Sam's id, then `library` with `groupIds` and `labels` or `meaning`. With Smart search off there are no labels, text or meaning to search; say so and offer `smart_settings`.
- Sharing: `sharing` lists your shared albums with their members, unread counts and the partner shares you offered or were offered, and the latest activity. `album_activity` (`albumId`, `cursor`) lists photos added, comments, likes and joins of one shared album, newest first. `archive` lists archived photos by month like `library`. `smart_status` shows your Smart features: the switches, what is analysed, queued, failed or waiting for a key per stage, what was spent and the estimate for the rest. `smart_models` (owner only) lists the models each Smart feature may use. `photo_edit_state` (`itemId`) shows a photo's last edit and `canSaveVersion`. `photo_edit_job` (`jobId` from `video_edit`) shows a video edit's progress.

Pages carry a `next` value: pass it back as `cursor`. Ids from one read are what `drive_edit` takes.

## Change

`drive_edit` takes an `action`:

- Folders and files: `new_folder` (`parentId`, or `rootId` alone for the top of a drive, and `name`), `rename` (`itemId`, `name`), `move` and `copy` (`itemIds`, `toFolderId`), `star` (`itemIds`, `starred` false removes it), `trash` and `restore` (`itemIds`). Each answers with what was done, what failed and the names that already exist; read it and report the failures. A name that exists is never overwritten.
- Albums: `album_create` (`album` with kind manual or smart, `title`, `surface`, `scope`), `album_update` (`albumId`, `changes`), `album_add` and `album_remove` (`albumId`, `itemIds`). Deleting an album never deletes its files.
- `folder_hide` and `folder_show` (`folderKey` and `folderName` from `photo_folders`) hide a folder from the Library and the other photo views, or show it again. It only changes what Photos displays: backup of that folder keeps running and nothing moves or is deleted. Backup choices stay in Settings › Photos › Backup › Albums and folders.
- `keep` (`source` cameras or ai, `itemIds`) keeps camera snapshots or Made with AI pictures in the Shared Drive so they stay.
- `attach_to_chat` (`itemId`; a view only file shared with you is refused) shows you one PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP picture up to 4 MB so you can look at it. For a larger photo or any other file, say so and offer a link or open it for the person with `widget_show` kind photos.
- `version_restore` (`itemId`, `versionId`) brings back an earlier version; the current one is kept as a version.
- `share_create` (`share` with `itemId`, `permission` view or download, `expiresAt` or null, `password` or null) answers with the link, its end time and how to revoke it; tell the person all three, and suggest an end date unless they want none. `share_revoke` (`shareId`) stops a link at once. Never make a link the person did not ask for.
- `share_people` (`itemId`, `recipientId` (a person id from `person_read`), `role` view or edit, or none to stop sharing, optional `expiresAt` for when the access ends by itself) shares a file or folder of your own drive with a person; a folder covers everything inside it, and only you, the drive's person, share it. This is different from `share_create`, which makes a public link.
- Comments: `comment_add` (`itemId`, `body`, `threadId` to reply, `mentions` person ids who can open the item), `comment_edit` (`commentId`, `body`, `mentions`; only your own), `comment_resolve` (`commentId`, `resolved` false reopens), `comment_remove` (`commentId`). Anyone who can open a file can comment; a comment mentioning a person without access is refused.
- Shortcuts: `shortcut_create` (`itemId` of the target, `name`, and `parentId` for a folder or `rootId` for the top of a drive) shows one file or folder in another place without copying it; `shortcut_update` (`shortcutId`, `name`, `parentId` or `rootId`) renames or moves it; `shortcut_remove` (`shortcutId`) removes only the shortcut, never its target.
- Access: `access_request` (`itemId`, `role` view or edit, `message`) asks the owner of an item you cannot open for access. `access_answer` (`requestId`, `decision` approve with `role` and `expiresAt` or null, or decline) answers a request waiting for you; approving shares the item.
- File requests: `file_request_create` (`folderId`, `title`, `message`, `expiresAt` or null) makes an upload link that drops what people send into that folder without letting them see it; it answers with the link, so tell the person the link and its end. Only make one when the person asked, and only when the owner allows members to share links. `file_request_update` (`requestId`, `title`, `message`, `expiresAt`) and `file_request_close` (`requestId`) change or stop it; what arrived stays.
- `lock_set` (`itemId`, `locked`) makes a file or folder view only: people it is shared with can open it in the app but cannot download, copy, sync or take it out, and nothing stops a screenshot. It does not limit you, the drive's person, or the Shared Drive.
- `folder_colour` (`itemId` of a folder, `colour` one of the palette names or null to clear) sets the colour only you see.
- `transfer_offer` (`itemId`, `recipientId`) offers a file or folder of your own drive to another person to own; they accept with `transfer_answer` (`transferId`, `accept`) and it moves into the top of their My Drive keeping its history, comments and links, and you keep edit access. `transfer_cancel` (`transferId`) takes back an offer not yet answered. Offer only when the person asked.
- `notifications_read` (`ids`, or `all` true) marks inbox entries read. `offline_set` (`clientId` of one of your phones, `itemId`, `on` false to stop) keeps a file or a whole folder available offline on that phone; a view only file cannot be kept offline.
- `placement_set` (`placement` with `deviceId`, `rootId`, `folderItemId` or null for the whole drive, `localPath` (the folder on the computer), `enabled`, `excluded`, `ignorePatterns`, `conflictCopies`) syncs a drive folder both ways with a folder on one of your computers; a folder shared with view only is synced read only. `mount_set` (`mount` with `deviceId`, `enabled`) turns that computer's mount on or off: always your whole My Drive, in its network drive place, nothing to choose; `placements` reports it as `mountPoint`. `placement_remove` (`placementId`) stops a sync or removes the mount; files on the computer stay.
- `configure` (`rootId`, `driveChanges` with `versions`, `skipPatterns`, `includeHidden`) changes a drive's settings, and `scan` (`rootId`) re-reads a drive from disk. You change your own drive; only the owner changes the Shared Drive. `conflict_resolve` (`conflictId`, `resolution` keep_hub, keep_client or keep_both) settles a sync conflict, and `scrub_start` (owner only) checks every file against its stored hash.

- People and pets: `person_update` (`groupId`, any of `personName` (null clears it), `linkedProfileId` (a person id from `person_read`, null unlinks), `hidden`) names a person or pet, links a person to a household member or hides the group. `person_merge` (`groupId` stays, `sourceGroupIds` fold into it) merges groups that are the same. `person_remove_faces` (`groupId`, `faceIds` from `item_faces`) tells the hub those faces are not that person; they never return. `person_dismiss_merge` (`groupId`, `otherGroupId`) stops the merge suggestion for two groups.
- Shared albums: `album_members` (`albumId`, `members` of `profileId` and `role` contributor or viewer, or none to remove a person with the photos they added) invites people; they accept in the app or with `album_join` (`albumId`); `album_leave` (`albumId`) leaves and takes your photos out. `album_comment` (`albumId`, `body`, optional `itemId` for one photo) and `album_like` (`albumId`, optional `itemId`) react. `album_activity_delete` (`albumId`, `activityId` from `album_activity`) deletes your own comment or like and cannot be undone: destructive. A link to a whole album is `share_create` with `share` holding `albumId` instead of `itemId`; it opens the album, not your drive.
- Partner sharing: `partner_set` (`recipientId`, `scope` all or people, `groupIds` when people, `fromDay`) offers a partner all your photos or the photos of chosen people from a day on, or changes the offer; the partner accepts with `partner_accept` (`shareId` from `sharing`) and `partner_stop` (`shareId`) ends it from either side. `partner_save` (`shareId`) with `itemIds` saves chosen partner photos into your library, or with `autoSave` true or false starts or stops saving every new one.
- Editing: `photo_edit` (`itemId`, `recipe`, `saveMode` version or copy) saves the whole edit as a recipe: `crop` (a box of fractions) or null, `quarterTurns` 0 to 3, `straighten` degrees, `flipHorizontal`, `flipVertical`, `light` (exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks), `colour` (saturation, warmth, tint), `filter` (a preset and a strength 0 to 100) or null, and `patches`. Sliders run from -100 to 100 and 0 changes nothing. Always send the whole recipe with every field, even untouched ones. `version` saves over the photo and keeps the original as a version; `copy` saves a new photo beside it; a photo that cannot be saved over is refused as a version with the reason, so save it as a copy and tell the person. `photo_revert` (`itemId`) restores the original from before the edit: destructive. `photo_erase` (`itemId`, `strokes` of `size` and `points` as fractions 0 to 1 of the picture) erases the brushed area with an image model and answers a `patchId` and `box`; put them into the `patches` of the recipe you then save with `photo_edit`. `video_edit` (`itemId`, `trim` of `startMs` and `endMs` or null, `stabilize`, `saveMode`) trims or steadies a video in the background and answers a job id; it needs `trim`, `stabilize` or both.
- Organising: `photo_meta` (`itemIds`, then `takenAt` to set the date, or `shiftMinutes` to move every date by that many minutes, and `latitude` with `longitude` and optional `locationLabel` to set the place or `clearLocation` true to remove it) changes how Photos shows date and place; the file itself stays untouched. `archive` and `unarchive` (`itemIds`) hide photos from Library and map or bring them back; they stay in albums and search. `locked_move_in` (`itemIds`) moves photos into the Locked folder, out of every other place including Drive, search, shares and every AI read: destructive for what the AI can reach afterwards. Moving photos back out and opening the Locked folder are the person's, with their PIN, in the app; never offer to unlock it and never try to read it.
- Smart features: `smart_settings` (`smartSearch`, `faceGrouping`) turns Smart search and tags, or Face grouping, on or off for you; turning one off stops its work and deletes what it stored. Turn one on only when asked: Smart search sends previews to OpenRouter. `smart_reanalyse` (`estimateMicros` from `smart_status`, owner only) analyses the whole library again and costs money; tell the owner the estimate first and send it only after they agree. `smart_retry_failed` reruns your failed photos (the owner's: every failed photo, which costs money; say so first).

There are no extra drives and no folders to add: a person has their own drive and the home has the Shared Drive. A member's AI makes a share link only when the owner allows members to share; a refusal is final, so say which rule applied.

## What is not possible here

The Locked folder is human-only: the AI cannot read it, unlock it, set or change its PIN or move photos out of it, and says so when asked. Choosing which models Smart features use and the owner's other Settings are changed with `operation_call` by the owner only. There is no separate “ask my photos” tool: answer a question about the photos with `people`, `library`, `item_faces` and `attach_to_chat`.

Permanent removal is not an AI action: `trash` moves files to Trash and only the person can empty it or delete for good in the app. The AI does not browse or write to Google Drive or Google Photos; the optional copy of a Google account is started by the person in the app.

## Show it

`widget_show` kind photos (`view` item, album or month, and `id`) puts a photo, an album or a month under your reply. Use ids from `drive_read`, and only when the person wants to see it, not for every search.

## Drive settings

`settings_read` lists `drive_settings` under changed_by. Upload expiry, member sharing, duplicate hashing and indexing are changed with `operation_call` updateDriveSettings (owner only); read the current values with readDriveSettings first and send only the fields that change.
